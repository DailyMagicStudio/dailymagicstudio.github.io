1. Personal Information We Collect

We collect information automatically, from you directly, or from third-party sources:

  • Account Information: name, username, email, password, profile picture, social media connections.
  • Technical & Usage Information: device type, IP address, OS version, clickstream data, cookies, advertising IDs.
  • Payment Information: order history, payment method (processed via third-party providers).
  • Support & Surveys: correspondence, game activity, survey responses, promotional event participation.
  • Contacts: if you import contacts to invite others to the Services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve our Services and provide support.
  • To personalize your experience and suggest connections.
  • To send administrative messages, updates, and marketing communications (if consented).
  • To conduct surveys, research, and promotional events.
  • To prevent fraud, enforce our terms, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers (analytics, hosting, customer support, marketing)

We may display ads and use third-party networks for analytics and marketing. You may opt-out via:
